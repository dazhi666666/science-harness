VISION, WITH A CLEAR STARTING POINT

Built today. Open for tomorrow.

IMPLEMENTED · v0.1

A working foundation

  • Configurable roles, runtime delegation, and authorized cross-branch result exchange.
  • Persistent operations, request deduplication, resource reservations, cancellation, and restart reconciliation.
  • A public Provider SDK, reference adapters, and a separately installed external computation plugin.
  • A local dashboard and a preserved real-model research demonstration.

Verified scope: one machine, one management domain, trusted plugins. The research demo is evidence of connectivity, not general scientific competence.

DIRECTION · NOT YET DELIVERED

Bring the loop into the lab

  • Connect a real instrument or a bounded experimental workstation.
  • Manage multi-step experiments while preserving resources between steps.
  • Package reusable domain methods, roles, and analysis as plugins.
  • Integrate robotics, navigation, dexterous manipulation, and IoT through existing controllers.

These need their own implementations and evidence. Unknown operations retain resource reservations; a cancellation request alone never proves equipment has stopped.
